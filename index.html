OS Concept Cheat-Sheet Viva Q&A
Q: Why Counting Semaphore?
Unlike a Mutex (binary 0/1), a Counting Semaphore manages $N > 1$ identical resources concurrently without race conditions.
Q: What is the unique feature?
The 4-stage lifecycle: Available → Charging → Completed → Released, mapping physical charger hardware states cleanly to OS thread life.
Q: What happens if 2 cars request the SAME charger in parallel?
Mutual Exclusion (Mutex) arbitrates: Thread 1 enters the Critical Section, while Thread 2 is BLOCKED. When Thread 1 finishes and calls release(), Thread 2 is signaled and unblocked.
